The upgraded space-themed flyer releases this week on the Atari VCS Veteran game developer Jani Penttinen has two games on the Atari VCS, Guntech and Utopos . This week he is releasing a revamped update for Guntech , Guntech 2.0 this week. The title is in the VCS store for $19.99 USD and will be a free update for people who own the original Guntech.
