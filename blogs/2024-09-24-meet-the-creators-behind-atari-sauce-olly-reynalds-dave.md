Olly Reynalds, known in the chilli world as Chilli Olly, and Dave Perrett, aka Chilli Pepper Perrett, both grew up in the golden era of gaming. They spent their childhoods mastering the Atari 2600 and now, years later, they’ve channelled that nostalgic love into their latest culinary project—Atari® Sauce (available at https://atarisauce.com/ ). Olly, a multi-award-winning chef and entrepreneur, has built a reputation for pushing culinary boundaries with his fiery creations and entrepreneurial ventures like Sauce Shed .
