Three new titles from the Aery series are coming to the Atari VCS, beginning with Aery — A Journey Beyond Time which lands in the VCS store Friday, August 27 Aery’s gameplay lets you experience the joy of gliding through the air, as you bank and swoop through an ever-changing array of lush landscapes. The games pull you along on a calming journey of discovery … blissful immersion at its best. Along the way you face new challenges and objectives, and in each title you encounter new characters and play new roles.
