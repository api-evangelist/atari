New York, NY (May 15, 2023) - Atari® announced the closing of the acquisition of Nightdive Studios, a full service game development and publishing company based in Vancouver, Washington. Nightdive has expertise in restoring, optimizing, and publishing classic video games. The studio has published over 100 titles and has garnered critical acclaim for their releases of seminal industry and fan-favorite titles including System Shock , Doom 64 , and Quake .
