New York, NY (October 24, 2023) - Atari® — one of the world's most iconic consumer brands and interactive entertainment producers — announced today the acquisition of multiple titles from Ronimo Games including Awesomenauts and Swords & Soldiers . The purchase includes the games, trademarks, and the underlying property. “The team at Ronimo developed a well-deserved reputation for character and game design,” said Atari CEO Wade Rosen.
