Torrance, CA (September 18, 2023) – My Arcade® teams up with the legendary Atari® brand to introduce the Atari® Gamestation Pro with 200+ games! The Atari® Gamestation Pro is available for pre-order today , at select retailers for delivery by October 31, 2023 for an MSRP of $99.99 USD . The Atari Gamestation Pro (DGUNL-7012) is the first console on the market to integrate the Atari 2600, 5200, 7800, Arcade and bonus titles into a single platform.
