Porsche Cars Australia recently released a campaign featuring two Porsche Taycan electric sportscars playing an intense, high-speed, real-life version of Pong. In this life-sized adaptation of the 1970's Atari classic, the paddles are two Porsche Taycan sportscars while the ball is a fast moving high-tech drone. Along with the video, Porsche Cars Australia have also created Taycan Arcade, an interactive web-based game inspired by Pong.
